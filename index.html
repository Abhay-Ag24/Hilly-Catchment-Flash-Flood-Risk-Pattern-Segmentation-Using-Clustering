Critical Limitations, Sampling Biases & Ethical Use Framework

Objective 3 Requirement

1. Orographic Rain-Gauge Sparsity & Spatial Sampling Bias

India Meteorological Department (IMD) telemetry weather stations are historically installed in accessible valley floors, district headquarters, and river confluences. In high-altitude terrain (>2,500m), rain gauge density drops sharply. Consequently, micro-scale convective cloudburst events occurring across isolated mountain ridgelines can remain undetected or smoothed out in 0.25° gridded products, creating a false perception of safety in remote upper catchments.

2. SRTM 30m Digital Elevation Model Limitations

While NASA/USGS SRTM 1-arc-second (~30m) elevation data offers global coverage, the 30m horizontal resolution tends to smooth incised bedrock gorges, ephemeral chutes, and micro-debris chutes. Furthermore, radar shadow, geometric layover, and foreshortening on extreme Himalayan cliff faces (>45°) introduce localized interpolation noise into derived slope and Topographic Wetness Index (TWI) values.

3. Ethical Deployment & Algorithmic Justice

Preventing Discriminatory Disinvestment: Designating a hilly catchment as "Tier 1: Extreme Risk" must not be leveraged by administrative bodies or insurance underwriters to justify systematic abandonment, insurance blacklisting, or denial of infrastructure subsidies to vulnerable tribal and montane subsistence farming communities. Instead, cluster designations must mandate affirmative government investment in decentralized automated sirens, community check-dams, and agroforestry terracing.

4. Early Warning Calibration: False Alarms vs. Lethal False Negatives

A flash flood in a 2-hour response time catchment gives zero margin for operational hesitation. While false negatives cause catastrophic casualties, excessive false alarms induce public cynicism and warning disregard. Operational deployment must treat cluster boundaries not as binary tripwires, but as Bayesian prior distributions that condition real-time Doppler radar and telemetry alerts.
